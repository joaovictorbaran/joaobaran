**Emotional tone:** [e.g. wonder, tension, quiet confidence, uncertainty]

## Image-generation prompt

Create a sophisticated, photorealistic cinematic editorial cover for an article about **[core idea]**. The image should evoke **[emotional tone]** through **[visual metaphor]**. Show **[protagonist]** in **[setting]**, composed **[placement/viewpoint/framing]** with one clear focal point and only a few purposeful supporting details. Use a restrained palette of deep blue and warm amber, with natural, motivated lighting and atmospheric depth. Use selective focus / shallow depth of field **[if useful to this concept]**. The image should feel like specific, art-directed editorial photography: believable, tactile, elegant, and not generic stock art. Horizontal **[16:9 or requested ratio]** crop suitable for an article cover. No text, lettering, logos, watermarks, clutter, or unrelated props.
